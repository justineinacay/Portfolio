05 HOW I THINK

How I approach the work.

A marketing process should have enough structure to be dependable, and enough room to learn what really works.

SCROLL THROUGH MY APPROACH ↓
01 / DISCOVER

Understand the problem.

Start with the audience, the business goal, and the evidence already available. Clarify what success would actually mean.

02 / DESIGN

Shape the journey.

Choose the right message, channel, creative direction, and next action. Make every element work toward the same outcome.

03 / EXECUTE

Build and launch.

Coordinate the moving parts: creatives, platforms, teams, schedules, and the systems that connect them.

04 / IMPROVE

Learn, then refine.

Use measured results to improve the work. Keep the parts that help, question what doesn't, and make the next cycle stronger.
