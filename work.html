01 / BRAND IDENTITY

A whole world, not just a logo.

Dulce & Banana

The goal was to express a warm, welcoming café identity that could feel consistent across printed materials, packaging, menus, and a physical storefront.

The challenge
Create a recognizable look across many customer touchpoints.
The approach
Develop a gold, ivory, and charcoal palette, editorial typography, brand motifs, and practical applications.
