PERFORMANCE THINKING

A cheap lead is not always a good lead.

Why lead quality, conversion paths, and sales follow-through matter as much as the price of an inquiry.

EDITORIAL DRAFT · NOT YET PUBLISHED AS A PERSONAL ARTICLE

Cost per lead is useful. It is not a complete picture of acquisition performance. If a campaign produces cheap contacts who never become qualified conversations, the apparent efficiency can hide a more expensive process downstream.

A more helpful review includes lead relevance, landing-page clarity, response speed, follow-up processes, and conversion into meaningful business outcomes. Even simple CRM hygiene can change the quality of what a marketing team learns.

For a marketer, the question is not just “How low can we get the CPL?” It is “What happened after someone raised their hand?”
