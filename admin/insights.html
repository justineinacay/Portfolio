Understanding these numbers

Estimated sessions are based on temporary, per-tab IDs—not persistent personal identifiers. Returning visitors may count as new sessions. Bots and blocked tracking can also affect totals.

For optional country-level and independent unique-visitor estimates, connect Cloudflare Web Analytics and open its private analytics dashboard.

Metrics only begin when your site and dedicated backend are configured and deployed.
